Bringing cognitive science to user experience design.

I'm a linguist-in-training at Stanford University, and a user experience engineer focused on design technology and human behavior. I'm passionate about building tech experiences that are human-centered, and utilizing my cognitive science training to inform design decisions.

At Stanford, I did a B.S. in Human-Computer Interaction (via the Symbolic Systems Department), and I'm finishing my M.A. in linguistics, where I research how people utilize features of language to indirectly communicate about emotions. After graduation, I want to continue my career in UX engineering, research, and design at the intersection of human cognition and technology.

Based in the San Francisco Bay Area. I'm a generalist at heart and open to any role in which I can influence user experiences -- engineering, design, research, etc.
